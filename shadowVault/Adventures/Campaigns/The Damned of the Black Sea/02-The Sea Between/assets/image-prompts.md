# Image-Prompts für generative KI

Zwei Beschreibungen zum Einfüttern in eine Bild-KI (Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion o.ä.). Beide bewusst spielerfreundlich gehalten: die Falschheit ist angedeutet, nie bestätigt.

## 1. Der alte Seemann (der Fährmann)

> A very old fisherman standing at the helm of a small wooden sailing ship at night, one weathered hand resting on the tiller. He wears a heavy, ancient coat of tarred canvas of no particular era, black and salt-stained (not a modern yellow raincoat). His beard is the grey of morning dew, long and unkempt; his face is deeply lined and weather-beaten, with calm, patient eyes the shifting color of sea weather. His hands are noticeably large and heavy for his thin wrists. Beside him hangs an old ship's lantern that glows with a warm flame carrying a faint, slow bluish pulse at its heart — the only warm light in the scene. Behind him, cast on the patched brown sail, his shadow is subtly too large, its outline not quite matching him — easy to miss, easy to dismiss. The sea around the boat is unnaturally smooth and grey, blending into fog at the horizon. Mood: quiet, melancholic, gentle but uncanny. Muted grey-blue palette with a single warm lantern glow. Painterly dark fantasy illustration, atmospheric, soft focus on the fog.

## 2. Die Halcyon (das Fischerboot)

> A small, weathered two-masted wooden fishing ketch sailing alone on a vast, unnaturally calm grey sea — absurdly small for open water. Her single patched brown sail is drawn taut and pulling steadily although the sea and air are dead still: the water around her is smooth as glass, without wake or ripple, mirroring the hull. Peeling paint in patterns like tide lines, weeping seams, rigging old and mended many times. At the stern, a lone lantern burns with a warm flame holding a faint slow bluish pulse, casting a small circle of light on the deck — the only warm color in the image. No crew visible on deck, or at most the silhouette of a lone old helmsman. Thick fog banks close off the horizon in every direction; no sun, no stars, a directionless grey twilight. Mood: eerie stillness, a ship that should not be able to sail and does anyway. Muted grey-blue palette, painterly dark fantasy seascape, wide shot with the ketch small against the fog.

## Hinweise zur Verwendung

- **Stil-Suffixe** je nach KI anhängen, z.B. `dark fantasy book illustration, muted palette, volumetric fog` oder für Midjourney `--ar 3:2 --style raw`.
- Beide Prompts vermeiden bewusst jede Bestätigung des Geheimnisses: der Schatten ist nur *„subtly too large"*, das Segel zieht nur *„although the air is dead still"* — Details, die Spieler wegerklären können.
- Für spätere Nächte (Verfall): dem Halcyon-Prompt anfügen: *"the ship visibly decayed, deck shortened, sail patched with cloth from nowhere, the lantern's circle of light shrunk to a single step around the helm."*
